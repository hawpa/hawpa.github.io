about us

who are we?

The National Alliance is a political and intellectual movement founded in Hawpa believes that Kurdistan must be for the Kurds — defined by a common ethnicity, a common language, and a common culture.

We are against both leftist and liberal ideologies, because both have destroyed the Kurdish nation and left Kurdistan without a nation-state.

Our basic goal is to create a prosperous national state — the Third Kingdom of Kurdistan — that will succeed the Kingdom of King Mahmoud and the Kurdish independence movement.

"A nation is a blood relationship, having a common language, and a common culture."

— hawpa manifesto 2025
Principles of Hawpa

Six main principles

1

selfsufficient nation

A nation is an organic spontaneous entity consisting of common blood, language, and culture. Cannot be created or changed externally.

2

One party state

The spontaneous state requires a single decision-making body. Multipartyism has been the backbone of mechanical and artificial states, not self-sustaining nations.

3

Closed economy

Protecting the domestic economy from foreign competition. Kurdish companies must remain under state protection.

4

Racial purity

The blood of the Kurdish nation must remain pure. The racial mixing of the spontaneous nation will be destructive.

5

Expansionism

A self-sufficient nation is always trying to expand its borders. Kurdistan must reach the level of an empire.

6

Kurditsan For Kurds

Kurdistan territory must belong to the Kurdish nation. No other nation has the right to live on Kurdish territory.

Ideology

What Are we against? what are we for?

Against leftism

The Kurdish left has been against the Kurdish state since its inception. The Kurdistan Communists supported the Iraqi government against the September Revolution. It disrupted the class struggle of the Kurdish nation and made fratricide common..

Against liberalism

Liberalism pitted individualism against socialism. That is what has undermined national identity. The Kurds have never read the individual — the Kurds have always been socialists.

For national socialism

Nationalism is an ethnic movement that seeks to create a state. The Kurdish nation must define itself in a coherent manner, not attach itself to mechanical states.

For national Hawpa

The national Hawpa (PSNK) is the one-time bringing together of all Kurds under one political framework — not according to class, but according to race, language, and culture.
